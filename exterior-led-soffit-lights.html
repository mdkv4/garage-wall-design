Installation Instructions

  1. CAUTION: Do not bend the strip.
  2. CAUTION: Keep metal away from the open contacts. Do not let the strip touch itself.
  3. Install the strip in an aluminum cooling profile.
  4. Do not operate the strip on its reel.
  5. Seal each cut (IP65 sleeve).
  6. In WLED, set the LED type to SK6812/WS2814 RGBW, with BGR + W&G swap.
  7. In WLED, set the current limiter to 50 mA for each zone and 20,000 mA maximum.
  8. Use the W channel for white light. Do not use 100% RGB white.
  9. CAUTION: Keep the PSU output at 24.0 V.
  10. Install the Dig-Quad in an enclosure with vents.
  11. Seal the hole where cables F1, F2 and CH1 go from the garage attic into the house. Use fire-stop material.

  1. Feed rule (QuinLED): the maximum distance between feeds is 10 m. Put one feed at each end. For 10 m, feed both ends. For 20 m, feed the start, the middle and the end.
  2. Four data channels give the maximum frame rate. Each channel controls one 2-strip section (≈270 zones). The longest channel sets the frame time. In the QuinLED example, ≈640 zones on one output gives ≈30 FPS.
  3. CH1 to F2 is the longest data cable (≈9 m), near the 10 m limit. There is no attic above the open corner of the L, so the cable cannot go in a straight line. Use the shortest attic route. If the cable length is near 10 m, install a data booster.
  4. The HEP-1000-24 (1,008 W) is not a Class 2 power supply. The electrician must confirm the cable rating and wiring method for W2, W6 and W9.
  5. Attic: the garage attic floor is on 2×4 sleepers (24" o.c., House Side to Street Side). There is a 1½" space above the truss chords for cables.

  1. WARNING: Make sure that a qualified electrician installs W1 and the AC connection. AC voltage can kill you.
  2. Power cable (W6, 2C): connect the + conductor to the +24V pad. Connect the − conductor to the GND pad.
  3. Data cable (W7, 2C): connect the DATA conductor to the DI pad. Connect the GND conductor of the data cable to the same GND pad as the power GND (①).
  4. At the controller, connect the data GND conductor to a − (GND) output terminal. The LED1–LED4 terminals carry data only. Connect each power cable to a fused + terminal and a − terminal.
  5. At the far end, connect only the power cable (W9: + and GND). Do not connect DO.
  6. At a corner, cut the strip on the copper pads. Solder a 3-conductor jumper (W8: 24V, GND, DATA).
  7. Install one fuse for each power feed (fuse values: feed table). LED1 and LED2 use the same fuse, and LED3 and LED4 use the same fuse. Use one terminal from each pair and the three other fused terminals.
  8. Connect all grounds together: PSU −V, Dig-Quad GND and strip GND.
  9. Put a ferrule on each wire end that goes into a screw terminal. Put heat-shrink tube on each soldered joint.
  10. Keep each data cable at 10 m or less. Keep data cables away from AC cables.
  11. Attach each cable at the soffit for strain relief. Make a drip loop where a cable goes from the attic into the soffit. Seal the hole.
  12. On the CN71 harness, find pins 5, 7, 8 and 9 with a meter. The wire colors are not in pin order.
  13. CAUTION: Remove the 10 unused contacts from the CN71 harness housing, or cut each unused wire and insulate it. A short on PV, PC or REMOTE can change or stop the PSU output.
  14. Use a relay module that operates with the 5.12 V Q1R trigger and has a dry contact (refer to the QuinLED relay guide).
  15. Make sure that the terminal names agree with the labels on your Dig-Quad board (refer to the QuinLED pinout guide).
